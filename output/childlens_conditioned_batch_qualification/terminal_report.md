**Terminal decision: `NO_GO_REQUIRES_USER_CHOICE`.**

The learner experiment may **not** begin. The sampler, causal physics
population, canonical media, deterministic reruns, and official learner
preprocessing smoke are complete. The terminal blocker is scientific: the
accepted batch fails all three frozen visual-distribution intervals, and a
bounded camera-nuisance repair overshoots motion and scene change before
persistence reaches its interval.

  maximum lift ranged from 0.099697 to 0.143199 m.
- A representative furnished+MPFB depth composite passed after a deterministic
  camera-follow repair removed pathological near-lens target scaling.
- Canonical furnished+MPFB videos and checksum receipts exist for all 24
  episodes. Four frozen-subset reruns reproduced every trace value exactly.
- The pinned official `facebookresearch/egobabyvlm` checkout at commit

These are simulator engineering and provisional ChildLens ages-3-to-5

## Why this is a terminal blocker now

Using the frozen V5 definitions at 1 Hz, the accepted 24-episode batch measured:

| Feature | Frozen ChildLens 90% interval | Generated mean | Gate |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Grayscale motion | 0.1095–0.1273 | 0.01635 | FAIL |
| Adjacent DINO persistence | 0.6912–0.7330 | 0.96843 | FAIL |
| Scene-change rate | 0.1069–0.1558 | 0.00000 | FAIL |

The persistence measurement uses the exact frozen local
`facebook/dinov2-small` frontend. It is a transparent model-model sensitivity,
not human validation or ground truth.

The smallest in-scope head-motion sensitivity increased viewpoint variation.
On the representative cell it produced motion 0.17017, persistence 0.81369,
and scene-change 0.27586. Thus motion and scene change crossed above their
intervals while persistence was still too high. Turning that nuisance up or
down cannot jointly match the three targets. The original accepted trace was
restored afterward.

The temporary external-disk disconnect was recovered after the user remounted
the same disk; incomplete renders resumed at two workers. It is not the
terminal blocker.

## Smallest user choice

Choose one scientifically different generator route before another batch:

1. authorize within-episode furnished-scene/content variation (bounded
   distractor motion and viewpoint transitions) while retaining the same
   object-centered activity family; or
2. reduce the qualification vector to timing/physics only, explicitly dropping
   the failed visual-distribution claim.

Option 1 is the smallest route that could plausibly decouple semantic
persistence from raw grayscale motion and scene-change frequency. It requires
new renderer/controller behavior and a newly frozen batch, so it is a material
scope expansion rather than another nuisance-parameter retry. Frozen
thresholds, ChildLens-only ancestry, and the learner-study boundary should
remain unchanged.
