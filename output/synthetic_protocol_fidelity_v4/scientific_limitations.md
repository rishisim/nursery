# V4 scientific limitations

V4 is a synthetic fixture qualification, not a scientific outcome run. Its hand-built one-hot-like observations, small fixed concept inventory, exactly balanced candidate bags, deterministic raw-stream simulator, and reused frozen v2 detector do not establish ecological validity, developmental plausibility, or transfer to BabyWorld/AEA data.

Passing controls shows only that the package's code paths are live and auditable. It does not show that a future development effect exists. The excluded-smoke seeds are permanently excluded from future development and confirmation. Any smoke accuracy is a software qualification measurement and must not be reported as evidence for the hypothesis.

The learner's exclusivity objective is a declared engineering objective inspired by cross-situational models; it is not claimed to reproduce a specific cognitive model or maximize a proven likelihood. The exact point-mass inference branch describes the empirical resampling distribution when variance is zero and explicitly does not estimate population uncertainty.

No restricted data, AEA material, v4 development outcomes, or confirmation outcomes are used in v4 qualification.
