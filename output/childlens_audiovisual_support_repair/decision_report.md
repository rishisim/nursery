# ChildLens audiovisual support repair decision

**Decision: `NO_GO_UNINFORMATIVE`**

After the external volume was connected and explicitly authorized for
read-only recovery, all exact private V5 artifacts were found and matched their
frozen public hashes. The outcome-blind stable balancing design was frozen and
committed before repaired score access, then applied exactly once.

All 378 participant-duration-lag arms passed support and nuisance balance:
minimum raw support 44, minimum ESS 43.74, maximum weight 0.0381, maximum
top-10 concentration 0.300, and post-weighting absolute SMD at most 0.10.
Participant coverage was 18/18 with six per fold. Precision, heterogeneity,
positive control, preprocessing, and cross-fold curve reproducibility passed.

The repaired primary zero-minus-long-lag contrast was 0.00177 with a
participant-clustered 90% interval of [-0.00266, 0.00620]. This is precise and
inside the unchanged ±0.02 primary equivalence margin. It does not pass the
detectable-structure gates.

The weak/flat terminal state also requires the participant-clustered 90% upper
bound on full-curve amplitude to be at most 0.02. The observed upper bound was
0.03713, so `PASS_PRECISE_WEAK_OR_FLAT` fails. This is not a negative alignment
claim: the instrument now gives a precise weak primary contrast, but the full
lag curve is not sufficiently flat under the unchanged equivalence gate.

No locked participant, AEA/BabyView corpus, new media, model retraining,
simulator generation, or side-cue condition was accessed or run. V1–V5 records
remain unchanged.

Michael Frank's bridge now has complete natural-distribution measurement and
an estimable development-only audiovisual calibration, but neither authorized
pass state. Locked confirmation is not justified and simulator fitting remains
blocked.
