This amendment is prospective and preserves every earlier NO-GO record
byte-for-byte. The fixed lever/sphere fixture remains an engineering sensor unit
test and is forbidden from the lexical corpus. No side-cue treatment arm is
authorized here.

## Frozen semantic design and gates

The exact prospective specification is
`configs/childlens_asset_rich_grounding_amendment.json`. It requires ten noun
categories with at least three split-disjoint visible instances per category,
eight event families, three layouts, two-to-five distractors, a visible
articulated neutral hand/forearm, and 20-second 640×360 30-fps wide-angle
chest-view episodes. Visible PBR meshes may use separate invisible convex
collision proxies.

All language is generated from the canonical event graph. The primary eventual
acquisition endpoint uses nonce labels in natural German carrier phrases, with a
familiar-noun ceiling audit. Anna is only the pilot voice; a bounded pinned
Piper comparison must occur before learner outcomes if feasible.

The historical `weak_alignment_seconds` values 0.00177 and 0.00620 are not
seconds. They are similarity-contrast estimates/confidence bounds. Because
ChildLens supplied no valid referential-lag estimator, alignment is frozen as a
simulator-controlled event-relative low/central/high sensitivity axis. The
later synchronized/shifted/shuffled/uninformative construction remains sealed.

The fidelity pilot is scored before distribution construction. Failure of any
required semantic/appearance, complex-contact, decoded-channel, deterministic,
or throughput gate terminates `NO_GO_ASSET_RICH_FIDELITY`. Only a passing pilot
permits the independent generator and participant-disjoint cross-fit stage.
