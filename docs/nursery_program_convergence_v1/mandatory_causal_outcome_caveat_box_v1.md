> **What this result would and would not show.** The outcome is a paired synthetic simulator test with simulator-oracle evaluation truth and side cues withheld at evaluation. ChildLens contributes only a local, model-derived, uncertainty-preserving, K=5-protected aggregate calibration envelope; pseudo-labels and model agreement are not human truth or human reliability. The result does not test ChildLens acquisition, raw audio/video learning, infant learning, naturalistic ecological validity, or BabyView generalization. Physical side streams absent from ChildLens remain simulator-defined counterfactuals. No AEA or BabyView empirical ancestry, cross-corpus pooling, learner weights, tokenizers, vocabulary, embeddings, or restricted payloads are licensed. A positive result supports only the tested simulator/learner mechanism; a valid non-positive result does not prove zero effect or general impossibility.
