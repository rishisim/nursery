## Decision

Reuse [synthetic weak-alignment recovery v1](../../output/synthetic_weak_alignment_recovery_v1/scientific_report.md) as a **provisional symbolic action-alignment mechanism proof**. Its development result has the right directional causal skeleton: synchronized event/null information is available during training, matched disrupted controls receive the same weak vision-language experience, and the final evaluator receives no side cue. On the held-out action-composition endpoint, synchronized training reached 95.57% versus 35.42% for group-safe shuffle, a +60.16 percentage-point paired contrast (95% CI +57.55 to +64.58; 12/12 development corpus/model pairs positive).

This reuse narrows rather than enlarges the claim. V1 is not a general lexical-acquisition proof, noun-object result, raw-perception result, confirmation outcome, naturalistic result, or infant result. The next missing test is the minimal calibration-conditioned sensitivity extension frozen in [frozen_calibration_conditioned_sensitivity_protocol.json](frozen_calibration_conditioned_sensitivity_protocol.json). No scientific outcome is authorized or has been run under that protocol.

## ChildLens boundary

The prior **CHILDLENS_LEXICAL_FEASIBILITY_STOP_CURRENT_RESOURCES** remains terminal. The lack of qualified human lexical validation is not repaired by model agreement, and ChildLens is not reopened as lexical ground truth or acquisition evaluation.

ChildLens does not measure IMU, contact, touch, proprioception, motor commands, or causal effect size; those remain explicitly simulator-defined counterfactuals. AEA and BabyView empirical ancestry remain excluded.

## Active extension

The active protocol reuses the verified weak-alignment event-bag and cue-free evaluation interfaces, then adds only:

- aggregate-range binding from the permitted ChildLens calibration receipt;
- factorized opaque noun/object and verb/action meanings;
- a genuinely corrective event-selection update shared by nouns and verbs; and
- fail-closed tests for pairing, ancestry, evaluation withholding, side-only leakage, and confidence-only sharpening.

Its five matched arms are absent-side baseline, synchronized side, group-shuffled side, balanced time-shifted side, and corrupted/uninformative side. All primary episodes, candidate bags, initialization, architecture, batch order, optimizer updates, stopping rules, and compute remain equal. Evaluation uses synthetic vision and language only; a physically separate simulator oracle supplies evaluation truth. The directional standard is a mean synchronized lift of at least 3 percentage points, a one-sided 95% corpus-level lower bound above zero, and at least 60% positive corpus effects, conjunctively for the frozen endpoints and comparators.
