# Research rationale: corrective weak alignment

Cross-situational evidence can support word learning despite ambiguous individual occurrences ([Yu and Smith, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2007.01915.x); [Fazly, Alishahi, and Stevenson, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1551-6709.2010.01104.x)), while multiple-instance learning provides a formal event-bag representation with latent responsible instances ([Dietterich, Lathrop, and Lozano-Perez, 1997](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-3702(96)00034-3)). Multimodal cues are therefore placed inside the event-plus-null alignment posterior, where they can alter semantic sufficient statistics, rather than applied after learning as confidence sharpening.

Training-only privileged-information work ([Karlsson et al., 2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v151/k-a-karlsson22a.html)) motivates the strict boundary: synchronized cues may shape the learned lexical state, but the deployed/evaluated model must use ordinary language/visual inputs alone. Composition splits hold atomic constituents in training while withholding their combinations ([Keysers et al., 2020](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SygcCnNKwr)), so success measures recombination rather than exposure to the answer. Proper scoring rules ([Gneiting and Raftery, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437)) describe the full distribution but remain secondary because the primary construct is acquisition of a correct ordering.

Corpus seeds randomize the synthetic population and are the unit of inference, with heterogeneous-group t summaries motivated by [Ibragimov and Müller (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1162/REST_a_00545). Model seeds randomize initialization and example order and are paired within corpus. The intersection-union rule ([Berger and Hsu, 1996](https://doi.org/10.1214/ss/1032280304)) encodes the narrow claim: synchronized cues must improve both acquisition and transfer relative to the stronger absent/shuffled comparator. Negative, disruption, oracle, exact-window, semantic, and null mutations make a causal interpretation falsifiable. The whole design is prospective rather than adapted to observed outcomes, following [Morris, White, and Crowther (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.8086).

The cited methods justify pieces of the design, not infant-language or ecological claims. No external dataset is downloaded or used.
