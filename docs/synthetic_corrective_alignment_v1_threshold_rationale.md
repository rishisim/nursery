# Prospective threshold rationale: corrective alignment v1

All thresholds below are frozen before construction qualification. They define whether the synthetic test has useful headroom, a working causal manipulation, and a practically meaningful acquisition effect; they are not estimated from construction or development outcomes. Morris, White, and Crowther (2019) motivates prospective simulation estimands and operating criteria; Gneiting and Raftery (2007) motivates retaining proper scores as descriptive distributional checks; Berger and Hsu (1996) motivates the conjunctive intersection-union rule; Ibragimov and Müller (2016) supports corpus-level t summaries under heterogeneous independent groups. Full primary-source URLs are in `synthetic_corrective_alignment_v1_primary_sources.json`.

| Frozen criterion | Value | Construct rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Negative/disrupted top-1 ceiling | 0.80 | Guarantees at least 20 percentage points of possible correction and at least 20% wrong/tied meanings; this directly excludes the prior already-perfect failure mode. |
| Oracle mean / one-sided LCB / lift LCB | 0.90 / 0.85 / 0.15 | Requires the learner/data to recover nearly all mappings with strong alignment and to improve materially over the stronger absent/shuffled baseline. These are positive-control criteria, not evidence for synchronization. |
| Primary minimum effect and LCB | +0.10 / >+0.10 | Ten top-1 points is the smallest effect considered scientifically useful for this small synthetic lexicon; requiring the uncertainty bound, not only the point estimate, to exceed it avoids reviving the old +0.005 confidence-gain convention. |
| Synchronized LCB above exact chance | >+0.20 | Prevents a comparator contrast from passing while absolute acquisition remains weak. |
| Positive-corpus / large-negative fractions | ≥0.60 / ≤0.20 at −0.10 | Predeclared sign diagnostics require broad support and limit materially adverse corpus strata without substituting for the primary confidence bound. |
| Equivalence margins | ±0.03 | Three top-1 points is small relative to the +0.10 practical effect and the 0.20 headroom requirement; absent, constant-zero, disconnected, and crossed selective mutations must fit wholly inside this band at 90% confidence. |
| Corrupted gain UCB | <+0.03 | A deliberately wrong channel may not show even a small credible benefit over absent. |
| Detector lift LCB, overall and present/null | >+0.10 | Requires synchronization to change event-or-null information by at least the primary practical scale before semantic outcomes are interpretable. |
| Side-only identity UCB above chance | <+0.10 | Rules out a side channel whose detector outputs alone classify lexical identity at an effect as large as the primary minimum. |
| High/low side-informativeness detector contrast | >+0.10 | Confirms the generator’s declared side-informativeness factor affects the detector rather than existing only as metadata. |
| Exact-window low-vs-high-lag drop / latent recovery | >+0.10 / >+0.05 | Requires temporal uncertainty to materially damage exact alignment and the latent mechanism to recover at least half the primary practical scale. It is a qualification gate and a required causal-attribution gate for any positive development result. |
| Null-head / null-side / null-update drops | mean ≥0.10 and LCB >0.05 / LCB >0.05 / LCB >0.05 | Establishes a nontrivial crossed null pathway. `null_side_zero` removes only synchronized null evidence while retaining learning; `null_update_disconnected` freezes the head at the prespecified initial prior 0.30 while retaining side evidence; post-fit `null_head_ablation` alone forces the head near zero. Semantic endpoints must remain within their frozen ±0.03 equivalence margins and present harm may be no worse than −0.03. |
| Semantic-side / alignment mutation drop LCBs | >0.03 / >0.02 | Positive controls require active side semantics and alignment order to matter, while allowing smaller qualification effects than the final +0.10 claim. They are required to attribute any positive development result. |
| Present/null equality tolerance | 1e-12 | Synchronized-minus-absent and synchronized-minus-shuffled present/null effect vectors within this numerical tolerance are treated as identical and fail; correlation is descriptive, while crossed mutations supply the causal evidence. |
| Hand disagreement active / mutations | ≥0.75 / ≤0.25 | A large 50-point separation across 32 prespecified cases makes it auditable that side evidence can correct a wrong unique top and that zero/disconnect/permutation/old-gate variants cannot. |
| Construction heterogeneity counts/ranges | 12 geometry and repetition digests, 8 splits, all 3 ambiguity strata, plus fixed realized-spread minima | These thresholds require most of 16 preallocated corpora to differ structurally and require realized—not identifier-derived—variation in event counts, grounded rate, ambiguity, lag, visibility, and detector quality. |
| Parallel speedup / resource contingency | ≥1.20× / 1.50× | A modest but real process-level gain is required; the 50% resource contingency is applied to observed peaks and projected full-cohort storage before four jobs are frozen. |

The 100-corpus development size and three within-corpus model replicates are justified separately in `synthetic_corrective_alignment_v1_sample_size.md`. A clean null remains valid whenever the oracle, negative, leakage, detector, and selective-path controls make the test interpretable.
