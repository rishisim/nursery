# ChildLens media-grounding environment protocol

This protocol was frozen before any new renderer installation, launch, render,
generator fitting, or output scoring. Its machine-readable authority is
`configs/childlens_media_grounding_environment.json`.

ChildLens is the sole empirical source. Only the existing participant-distinct
18-person development aggregate is admissible; the 22 locked participants
remain sealed. AEA and BabyView have no empirical, lexical, checkpoint, example,
or measurement role. Ages 3–5 support only provisional developmental
calibration, never an infant claim. The failed Qwen3-VL/Gemma categorical
referent gate and failed natural audiovisual V1–V5/support-repair result remain
immutable pre-outcome failures. Generative VLM referent labels and natural AV
repair are prohibited.

## Backend and environment decision

The sole primary route is official TDW v1.13.0 from GitHub. It receives one
isolated Apple-Silicon install/launch/render attempt and at most one documented
compatibility repair. The acceptance scene must deterministically expose RGB,
instance visibility or segmentation, depth when supported, collision-derived
contact, pose/joint state, and timestamps. Failure immediately selects the sole
fallback: official BlenderProc with Blender. No third engine and no
NVIDIA-only stack may be tried.

German waveform synthesis is fixed to the installed local macOS `say` voice
Anna (`de_DE`) at 175 words/minute. It is not a ChildLens voice and performs no
voice cloning. There is no post-score TTS fallback. The learner is the locally
pinned official egobabyvlm checkout at commit
`224621caf0628270b6115845ac75a65b984234a3`, using its CLIP+ and frame-text
interfaces on MPS if supported and CPU otherwise. No BabyView data or
checkpoint is admissible.

## Frozen measurement and validation

One timestamped episode event graph and physical state must drive rendering,
action, proprioception, collision/contact, and IMU. The renderer emits playable
24-fps MP4, a 24-kHz WAV, exact script/timestamps, RGB and instance visibility,
plus depth/segmentation where supported. The learner view is sampled at 1 fps.

The exact feature tiers, estimators, seeds, splits, support/distribution gates,
language and causal checks, anti-leakage rules, oracle/negative controls, and
fail-closed fallback policy are frozen in the machine-readable protocol.
Validators decode emitted files and may not read target-valued generator
metadata. ChildLens-measured observables are kept distinct from simulator-known
causal controls and deliberately varied, explicitly uncalibrated sensitivity
axes.

The bounded pilot has 12 episodes across the three frozen seeds. It is scored
once after generation. Any required failure produces
`NO_GO_MEDIA_GROUNDING_ENV`; it may not trigger engine, TTS, estimator,
threshold, split, or learner shopping. `MEDIA_GROUNDING_ENV_READY` requires all
gates, including official-interface smoke tests and the two readiness controls.
The final cue-lift arms are explicitly unauthorized.
