# Research rationale: scientific core v2 / operational package v3

Cross-situational evidence can support word learning despite ambiguous individual occurrences ([Yu and Smith, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2007.01915.x); [Fazly, Alishahi, and Stevenson, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1551-6709.2010.01104.x)), while multiple-instance learning provides a formal event-bag representation with latent responsible instances ([Dietterich, Lathrop, and Lozano-Perez, 1997](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-3702(96)00034-3)). Multimodal cues are therefore placed inside the event-plus-null alignment posterior, where they can alter semantic sufficient statistics, rather than applied after learning as confidence sharpening.

Training-only privileged-information work ([Karlsson et al., 2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v151/k-a-karlsson22a.html)) motivates the strict boundary: synchronized cues may shape the learned lexical state, but the deployed/evaluated model must use ordinary language/visual inputs alone. Composition splits hold atomic constituents in training while withholding their combinations ([Keysers et al., 2020](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SygcCnNKwr)), so success measures recombination rather than exposure to the answer. This is also why the original brief's “held-out concepts” language is narrowed explicitly: an opaque atomic label never exposed in training is not identifiable from a non-semantic event-boundary cue, whereas new instances of acquired mappings and new constituent-preserving compositions are identifiable transfer tests. Proper scoring rules ([Gneiting and Raftery, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437)) describe the full distribution but remain secondary because the primary construct is acquisition of a correct ordering.

Corpus seeds are independent, identically distributed draws from the one frozen synthetic generator and are the unit of inference. Their bounded contrasts support a studentized central-limit approximation at 100 corpora, with a paired corpus bootstrap as sensitivity analysis. [Ibragimov and Müller (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1162/REST_a_00545) motivates reducing heterogeneous independent groups to group-level summaries; its finite-cluster theorem is not asserted to apply directly to these corpus effects. Model seeds randomize initialization and example order and are paired within corpus. Operational package v3 rechecks that those seeds produce distinct initial states, epoch-order sequences, and learned semantic/null states with seed metadata excluded, and that top-1 metrics vary in at least half of the frozen rows. It also rechecks the realized 100-corpus heterogeneity contract before efficacy; inadequate structure yields `REVISE`, while malformed or incomplete units abort without publication. The intersection-union rule ([Berger and Hsu, 1996](https://doi.org/10.1214/ss/1032280304)) encodes the narrow claim: synchronized cues must improve both acquisition and transfer relative to the stronger absent/shuffled comparator. Negative, disruption, oracle, exact-window, semantic, and null mutations make a causal interpretation falsifiable. The whole design is prospective rather than adapted to observed outcomes, following [Morris, White, and Crowther (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.8086).

Process parallelism is an execution property rather than a scientific factor. Coupled conditions remain inside one corpus×model bundle, model-seed waves preserve the frozen pairing structure, and canonical parent-only reduction preserves the exact statistical order. A narrow per-worker code/input/startup commitment plus full parent pre/post verification keeps verification bounded, while independent validation binds every request root, digest, and capability envelope. The planning estimate scales the complete measured parallel cohort—including parent validation, merge, scoring, and adjudication—and adds a worst-worker-wave stress allowance, completion-bound preservation timing, and launch-guard costs. Since the parent retains results for every unit, the RAM gate scales its entire observed micro-fixture high-water RSS by unit count. This intentionally overcounts static interpreter/library memory but cannot erase unit-dependent retention through high-water-history subtraction; separate physical- and currently-available-memory ceilings keep that conservative projection operationally bounded. Runtime telemetry and scheduler evidence remain excluded from scientific adjudication.

The cited primary-method pages were reverified on 2026-07-16. They justify pieces of the design, not infant-language or ecological claims. No external dataset is downloaded or used.
