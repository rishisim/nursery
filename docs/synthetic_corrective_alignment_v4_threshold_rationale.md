# Prospective threshold rationale: scientific core v2 / operational package v3

All thresholds below are frozen before construction qualification. They define whether the synthetic test has useful headroom, a working causal manipulation, and a practically meaningful acquisition effect; they are not estimated from construction or development outcomes. Morris, White, and Crowther (2019) motivates prospective simulation estimands and operating criteria; Gneiting and Raftery (2007) motivates retaining proper scores as descriptive distributional checks; Berger and Hsu (1996) motivates the conjunctive intersection-union rule. Ibragimov and Müller (2016) motivates first reducing heterogeneous independent groups to group-level summaries, but is not claimed as a direct theorem for the bounded corpus contrasts here. The inferential justification is instead the fixed generator's independent, identically distributed corpus draws, bounded corpus-level contrasts, and the studentized central-limit approximation at 100 corpora; the paired corpus bootstrap is a sensitivity check. Full primary-source URLs are in `synthetic_corrective_alignment_v4_primary_sources.json`.

| Frozen criterion | Value | Construct rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Negative/disrupted top-1 ceiling | 0.80 | Guarantees at least 20 percentage points of possible correction and at least 20% wrong/tied meanings; this directly excludes the prior already-perfect failure mode. |
| Oracle mean / one-sided LCB / lift LCB | 0.90 / 0.85 / 0.15 | Requires the learner/data to recover nearly all mappings with strong alignment and to improve materially over the stronger absent/shuffled baseline. These are positive-control criteria, not evidence for synchronization. |
| Primary minimum effect and LCB | +0.10 / >+0.10 | Ten top-1 points is the smallest effect considered scientifically useful for this small synthetic lexicon; requiring the uncertainty bound, not only the point estimate, to exceed it avoids reviving the old +0.005 confidence-gain convention. |
| Synchronized LCB above exact chance | >+0.20 | Prevents a comparator contrast from passing while absolute acquisition remains weak. |
| Positive-corpus / large-negative fractions | ≥0.60 / ≤0.20 at −0.10 | Predeclared sign diagnostics require broad support and limit materially adverse corpus strata without substituting for the primary confidence bound. |
| Equivalence margins | ±0.03 | Three top-1 points is small relative to the +0.10 practical effect and the 0.20 headroom requirement; absent, constant-zero, disconnected, and crossed selective mutations must fit wholly inside this band at 90% confidence. |
| Corrupted gain UCB | <+0.03 | A deliberately wrong channel may not show even a small credible benefit over absent. |
| Detector lift LCB, overall and present/null | >+0.10 | Requires synchronization to change event-or-null information by at least the primary practical scale before semantic outcomes are interpretable. |
| Side-only identity UCB above chance | <+0.10 | Rules out a side channel whose detector outputs alone classify lexical identity at an effect as large as the primary minimum. |
| High/low side-informativeness detector contrast | >+0.10 | Confirms the generator’s declared side-informativeness factor affects the detector rather than existing only as metadata. |
| Exact-window low-vs-high-lag drop / latent recovery | >+0.10 / >+0.05 | Requires temporal uncertainty to materially damage exact alignment and the latent mechanism to recover at least half the primary practical scale specifically in the prespecified high-lag stratum. `exact_window` is a separate temporal comparator, not a member of the absent/disrupted-channel headroom family. It is a qualification gate and a required causal-attribution gate for any positive development result. |
| Null-head / null-side / null-update drops | mean ≥0.10 and LCB >0.05 / LCB >0.05 / LCB >0.05 | Establishes a nontrivial crossed null pathway. `null_side_zero` removes only synchronized null evidence while retaining learning; `null_update_disconnected` freezes the head at the prespecified initial prior 0.30 while retaining side evidence; post-fit `null_head_ablation` alone forces the head near zero. Semantic endpoints must remain within their frozen ±0.03 equivalence margins and present harm may be no worse than −0.03. |
| Semantic-side / alignment mutation drop LCBs | >0.03 / >0.02 | Positive controls require active side semantics and alignment order to matter, while allowing smaller qualification effects than the final +0.10 claim. They are required to attribute any positive development result. |
| Present/null equality tolerance | 1e-12 | Synchronized-minus-absent and synchronized-minus-shuffled present/null effect vectors within this numerical tolerance are treated as identical and fail; correlation is descriptive, while crossed mutations supply the causal evidence. |
| Hand disagreement active / mutations | ≥0.75 / ≤0.25 | A large 50-point separation across 32 prespecified cases makes it auditable that side evidence can correct a wrong unique top and that zero/disconnect/permutation/old-gate variants cannot. |
| Hand null posterior / semantic-update L1 | ≥0.90 / ≤0.02 | In every prespecified all-null hand case, at least 90% mean posterior mass must go to null (at most 10% to candidate events), while at most 0.02 L1 mass may be redistributed in the semantic state. This is a deterministic pathway-isolation control: it demands strong null rejection and permits only a one-percentage-point total-variation semantic perturbation, rather than treating null evidence as weak semantic evidence. |
| Both signed shifts | synchronized-minus-shift LCB >0 for both −13 and +13 samples | Either temporal direction could accidentally preserve an asymmetric cue. Requiring a strictly positive one-sided lower bound in both directions establishes directional disruption; the separate +0.10 detector and exact-window gates carry the magnitude requirement, so this zero boundary is not an efficacy threshold. |
| Semantic-update disconnection | endpoint-drop LCB >0.05 for lexical and composition top-1 | Disconnecting semantic sufficient-statistic updates while retaining the remaining learner must cost at least five top-1 points, half the final practical-effect scale. This directly rules out a detector-only or confidence-only route while reserving the full +0.10 threshold for the scientific claim. |
| Lag strata | RMS boundary 8 samples; ≥4 high- and ≥4 low-lag corpora | RMS combines the independently drawn lag mean and spread. Eight samples is approximately one minimum-width event (event widths are 7–12 samples), so the high stratum represents genuine window displacement. Four of the 16 preallocated corpora in each tail is the minimum frozen guard against a one- or two-corpus comparator; the effect gates, rather than the count alone, establish temporal sensitivity. |
| Corrupted detector accuracy | exactly 0.0 | The corrupted control is generated to favor the wrong event or wrong null state. Any correct event-or-null selection therefore means the intended corruption was not realized and fails qualification; this is an exact manipulation invariant, not a fitted performance target. |
| Structural digest counts | ≥12/16 action geometries, episode geometries, and repetition patterns; ≥8/16 held-out splits; all 3 ambiguity strata | Twelve requires at least 75% of qualification corpora to be structurally distinct while allowing accidental digest equality; eight requires at least half the corpora to use different held-out compositions; all three causal ambiguity regimes must occur. These counts exclude a seed-invariant factor lattice without conditioning on endpoint success. |
| Realized discrete heterogeneity | ≥4 candidate-count histograms and ≥4 grounded-episode counts | Four distinct realized summaries is enough to reject a binary or constant construction artifact across 16 corpora without requiring every finite-sample count to be unique. |
| Action-geometry variation / separation | pairwise-distance range ≥0.02; self-versus-cross dot margin ≥0.25 | The 0.02 range forces measurable corpus variation in prototype geometry. The larger 0.25 within-corpus margin makes the independently generated action instances identifiable before asking whether words map to them; both primitive and manner tables must pass and be shared only through latent generator provenance, never learner-visible fields. |
| Realized continuous heterogeneity | grounded-rate range ≥0.05; foil-rate range ≥0.05; observed-lag-mean range ≥5 samples; visibility-peak range ≥0.03; detector-accuracy range ≥0.05 | Each minimum is a prespecified nontrivial fraction of its generator range (0.24 grounded rate, stratum-separated foil rates, 24-sample lag mean, 0.38 visibility, and 0.27 side-informativeness before noise). The five-sample lag range is also over half the high/low event-width scale. These are construction-distribution checks, not searched-for outcome effects. |
| Development scalar-factor diversity | ≥75 distinct in-range draws for each of 7 factors across 100 corpora | The v3 operational validity gate carries the construction cohort's 12/16 (75%) structural-diversity standard into the development cohort. It rejects constant, binary, or low-level factor lattices while allowing up to 25 accidental repeated values. Every draw must also remain within its frozen global or ambiguity-stratum-specific generator range. |
| Model stochasticity | sample SD ≥1e-6 in ≥50% of corpus×condition×top-1 rows, plus distinct initialization, epoch-order, and learned-state digests | The numerical floor is applied to the exact 2,700-row development inventory: 100 corpora × nine conditions × `lexical_acquisition_top1`, `heldout_composition_top1`, and `presence_balanced_accuracy`. Every row contains all three frozen model values and is bound by its recomputed mean to the matching model average; missing, duplicate, unexpected, or unbound rows abort publication. Package v3 also requires all three initial-state and epoch-order sequences to differ within every corpus and hashes only learned semantics/null rates when testing fitted-state distinctness, excluding model-seed metadata. Together these reject metadata-only or numerically irrelevant hash jitter; no efficacy claim is inferred from variation magnitude. |
| Parallel speedup / resource contingency | ≥1.20× / 1.50× | A modest but real process-level gain is required. For RAM, the complete parent high-water RSS is scaled by the development-to-micro unit ratio, four observed worker peaks are added, and the 1.50 factor is applied; the result must remain below 50% of physical and 85% of currently available RAM. The 85% transient-availability ceiling leaves at least 15% of measured reclaimable memory while the independent physical-memory ceiling and already-contingent projection prevent host overcommit; it was fixed prospectively because ordinary background variation made 75% and 80% ceilings operationally unstable. Storage is likewise projected to the full cohort before four jobs are frozen. |

All heterogeneity gates are applied to all 16 preallocated construction corpora, which are retained without search or replacement. The construction factor-draw audit requires more than one distinct draw for every frozen factor. Before a future development cohort can yield `GO` or scientific `STOP`, package v3 rechecks every invariant across all 100 corpora and preserves the construction distinctness proportions: 75 action/episode/repetition digests, 50 held-out splits, 25 candidate histograms and grounded counts, and the separately locked 75 distinct in-range values for each scalar factor, with unchanged continuous-range and geometry-separation thresholds. Valid but inadequate realized heterogeneity yields `REVISE`; malformed or incomplete evidence aborts publication. The 100-corpus development size and three within-corpus model replicates are justified separately in `synthetic_corrective_alignment_v4_sample_size.md`. A clean null remains valid whenever the oracle, negative, leakage, detector, and selective-path controls make the test interpretable.
