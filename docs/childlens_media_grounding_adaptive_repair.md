# ChildLens media-grounding adaptive repair amendment

This amendment preserves the earlier `NO_GO_MEDIA_GROUNDING_ENV` byte for byte.
It changes only the engineering-repair policy: ordinary dependency,
Python-version, architecture, controller, and renderer-interface repairs may be
iterated before any learner outcome exists. It does not reopen the failed
natural audiovisual or categorical-referent gates.

ChildLens remains the sole empirical source. The exact hashed 18-person
development allowlist is the only admissible calibration population; all 22
locked participants remain sealed. AEA and BabyView have no measurement,
example, vocabulary, media, checkpoint, or empirical-ancestry role. The age
claim is provisional for ages 3–5 and is never an infant calibration.

## Engineering selection before calibration

TDW 1.13.0.0 was repaired in Python 3.11.14 with setuptools 80.9.0. The
official universal build launched on the Apple M5 and completed one version
handshake. Repeated native arm64 and Rosetta x86_64 runs then connected but
blocked waiting for the initial ProcGenScene response. Because no physical
episode receipt exists, TDW is not a passing engine.

Blender 4.2.1 LTS arm64 is selected on physical capability and reliability
evidence, before generator fitting or learner outcomes. Its bounded scene uses a
24-kHz integer master clock, 240-Hz evaluated physics state, and 24-fps render
ticks. Contact is an exact signed-distance/intersection calculation on evaluated
oriented primitive collision geometry. Joint state and IMU are derived from
evaluated transforms, never action targets. Positive/negative contact fixtures
and dynamic/static signal controls pass. RGB, depth, segmentation, state,
actions, and playable MP4 are emitted.

Anna `de_DE` at 175 WPM remains the frozen local German TTS. A repeated
synthesis was byte-identical after canonical 24-kHz conversion, fully decodable,
and unclipped. The learner remains the pinned official egobabyvlm checkout.
Its imports, official transforms, MultiModalModel loss, backpropagation, and
tiny CPU/MPS evaluation pass.

## Scientific freeze and stop

The generator, estimators, split hashes, tuning budget, loss, and controls are
frozen in the machine-readable amendment before generator fitting. Tuning would
use two frozen six-person folds and assessment the third, rotated across all
three folds. Validators would decode emitted MP4/WAV and must not read declared
generator targets.

Execution stops before fitting because only the pooled public 18-participant
summary remains. The clean V5 receipt states that restricted feature material
was intentionally removed. A pooled summary cannot support honest
participant-disjoint tuning and assessment, and reconstructing fold statistics
from it would be fabrication. No calibration score, oracle control,
destroyed-mapping control, or cue-lift arm was run.
